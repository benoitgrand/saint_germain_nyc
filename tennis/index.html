Tournament Format

How the
tournament works

The Saint Germain Open is a two-week tennis tournament open to all members. Matches are played on outdoor hard courts in a relaxed but competitive spirit — one winning set, with time for a proper warm-up before each match.

Draws
Men's Singles
Women's Singles
Two separate draws — everyone competes in their category.
Court Surface
Outdoor
Hard Courts
Brian Watkins Tennis Center — all matches played outdoors.
Match Format
Two
Sets
Matches are played over two sets — expect 1h to 1h30 of play, within a 2-hour slot.
Time Slot
2-hour
Window
Each slot is 2 hours — enough for a warm-up and a full two-set match.
Equipment
Rackets & balls provided on site
A Saint Germain team member will be on site with equipment — but players are encouraged to bring their own racket and balls if they have them.

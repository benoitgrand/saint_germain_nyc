The Club

Paris meets
New York

Saint Germain is a private club founded to bring together French and New Yorkers around a shared desire: to live the city to the fullest, build genuine connections and share exceptional moments.

Whether you run Central Park at dawn, play tennis on weekends, or prefer rooftop parties at sunset — you'll find your community here.

We run every Thursday and Sunday at 6:30 PM, and the two locations alternate from one run to the next. The meeting point is shared in advance on Instagram and WhatsApp. Come as you are, run at your pace, stay for the drink.

How it works

Simple as
lace up & go

I

Show up

Every Thursday and Sunday at 6:30 PM. We recommend signing up to each run's event on Strava so you get the route and meeting point, also posted on Instagram. Everyone is welcome at the start.

II

Run together

All levels welcome. Groups form naturally by pace, so no one runs alone and no one gets left behind.

III

Stay for a drink

The best part. We finish in the West Village or the Upper East Side for a well-earned round.
